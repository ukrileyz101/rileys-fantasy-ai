Help & What's New

Answers to common questions, the latest announcements, and a look at what's coming next.

✦ Frequently asked questions

Who created Riley's Fantasy AI?

Riley's Fantasy AI is an independent creative project made by Riley Dunn, who wanted to build a place for people to create characters and explore fictional stories.

Will there be more updates?

Yes! The plan is to keep improving the app, adding new characters, creative tools, scenarios and design improvements. Check What's New for announcements. Dates and features may change.

Are chats unlimited?

There is no fixed chat-message limit built into the website itself. However, AI replies depend on the connected service, which may have usage limits, temporary outages or fair-use restrictions. Truly unlimited AI messages cannot be guaranteed.

Will there ever be a subscription?

The app is currently intended to be free to use. Optional paid features or subscriptions might be considered much later, but there is no confirmed subscription plan or launch date.

Can I create my own characters?

Yes. Open Character Studio to choose a name, personality, relationship, background and story scenario.

Can I combine story scenarios?

Yes. Character Studio lets you combine multiple story ideas, such as mafia boss, vampire royalty and werewolf hybrid.

Will my chats be saved?

Chats are stored locally in your browser. They may disappear if browser data is cleared or you use a different device. Use the backup feature in My Stories to keep a copy.

Is this a conversation with a real person?

No. Characters are fictional AI roleplays, even when inspired by real people. The AI does not represent or speak on behalf of a real individual.

Does adult content have its own controls?

Adult-themed preferences can be adjusted in Settings. They do not override the AI provider's content rules, and all intimate fictional scenarios must involve adults.
